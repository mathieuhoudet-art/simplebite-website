BRAZILIAN ROOTS.
YOUR EVERYDAY FAVORITE.

Pronounced ah-sah-EE, açaí is a deep-purple fruit from palm trees in the Amazon. Enjoyed in the region for generations, this plant-based fruit is loved for its distinctive flavor and nutritional value.

Often called a superfood, açaí fruit naturally contains fiber, unsaturated fats, vitamins, minerals, and antioxidant compounds that can contribute to a balanced, varied diet.

At Simple Bite, we bring this Brazilian favorite to Midtown Atlanta with creamy açaí, fruit, crunchy granola, and unlimited toppings. Try condensed milk and powdered milk for a Brazilian-style combination, or build your own. A little taste of Brazil, made your way.
